


# Destination Parameters

For each parameter, the destination should either give a concrete answer or say that the repo must have a good answer with a clear rationale documented in an ADR.

# Misc specifications

Each subsection under misc specifications needs to have a good answer specified in an ADR and summarized here.

## Purpose - What is the purpose of this repo, app, service, or system?

## Users - Who is this repo for, and what do those users need?

## Success criteria - What does success look like for this repo?

## Scope - What is inside the scope of this repo?

## Non goals - What should this repo explicitly not try to do?

## Constraints - What constraints must the repo respect?
